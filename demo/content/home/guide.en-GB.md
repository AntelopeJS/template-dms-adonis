## How to use this dashboard

1. **Open Helio** in the left menu: the dashboard sums up the activity, and each entry leads to a domain (sales, operations, invoices, monitoring, settings).
2. **Really act on it**: create a quote, move a deal through the pipeline, resolve a ticket, mark an invoice as paid. The KPIs follow.
3. **Switch to a phone**: shrink the window and the tables turn into cards.
4. **Open Modules** (bottom left): every module has its home page, and the store shows the ones you can add.
5. **Change language** in your profile: the whole interface and Helio's content follow.
