## Real-world cases

- **A platform for one client** — an installer, an estate agency, a firm: their data, screens, roles and emails, in days instead of months.
- **A vertical SaaS** — the same tool sold to dozens of companies in one sector, each in its own workspace, with its plans and billing.
- **An internal portal** — monitoring, tickets, planning and automations for a team, with fine-grained role permissions.
- **A growth tool** — measure usage, understand drop-offs, test a page variant, follow up by email.
